How to Revoke UPI Autopay / Bank Mandates

Even after canceling an app account, your bank or UPI app will keep debiting money unless you kill the active mandate. Here is how to do it in 30 seconds:

P

PhonePe

  1. Open PhonePe -> Tap your Profile Picture (Top Left).
  2. Scroll to 'AutoPay' under Payment Management.
  3. Select the active merchant (e.g. Hotstar, Spotify).
  4. Tap 'Remove AutoPay' & enter your UPI PIN.
G

Google Pay (GPay)

  1. Open Google Pay -> Tap Profile (Top Right).
  2. Tap on 'Autopay'.
  3. Select the subscription mandate.
  4. Tap 'Cancel Autopay' -> Confirm cancellation.
₹

Paytm

  1. Open Paytm -> Search 'Automatic Payments'.
  2. Tap 'UPI Automatic Payments'.
  3. Select the app or merchant subscription.
  4. Tap 'Cancel Automatic Payment'.
